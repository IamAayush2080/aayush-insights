✦ What I read · #7539

Reading List

Books, articles and resources that have shaped how I think. Not sponsored — just things I've genuinely found valuable.

📚 Books
🌌
A Brief History of Time
Stephen Hawking
The book that made cosmology accessible. Hawking explains the biggest questions — time, black holes, the origin of the universe — in a way that actually makes sense. Changed how I think about existence.
Cosmology
🧠
Thinking, Fast and Slow
Daniel Kahneman
Two systems in your brain — one fast and emotional, one slow and rational. Understanding this changed how I make decisions and why I'm wrong more often than I think.
Psychology
🔐
The Art of Invisibility
Kevin Mitnick
Kevin Mitnick was the world's most famous hacker. This book is about digital privacy — how exposed you really are and what to do about it. Essential reading for anyone interested in cybersecurity.
Cybersecurity
⚡
Atomic Habits
James Clear
Small steps compound into massive change. The system is more important than the goal. I don't agree with everything but the core framework of habit stacking and identity-based change is genuinely useful.
Self Improvement
🌊
Sapiens
Yuval Noah Harari
How Homo sapiens took over the world through shared myths, language and cooperation. Makes you question everything about civilization, money, religion and why humans are the way we are.
History · Philosophy
